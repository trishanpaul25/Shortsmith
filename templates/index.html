Paste a link. Get captioned clips.

Shortsmith finds the most exciting moments in a long video or a live stream, cuts them for Reels, Shorts or the feed, and burns in captions that light up word by word. It never downloads the whole video, only a small audio track and the few clips it picks. It runs on your own computer, and posts nothing unless you connect an account and ask it to.

How should it find the best moments?

Uses gemini-3.5-flash-lite (optimized to preserve API quota) with automated model fallback.
